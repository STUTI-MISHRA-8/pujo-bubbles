CHAPTER I✧MAHA ASHTAMI

A STORY FOR MY BUBBLES

When darkness
met the Divine.

A story of courage, fierce protection, and the kind of love that refuses to let darkness win.

☾

You know how much I love this time of year, but there’s one night in particular where the air feels different—thicker with magic, quiet with anticipation. It’s the story of Maha Ashtami.

A long, long time ago, the universe was falling apart. A demon named Mahishasura had tricked the gods. He possessed a shape-shifting form, half-man and half-buffalo, and he carried a spell that made him almost invincible: no god, no man, and no beast in existence could ever destroy him. Driven by arrogance, he conquered the heavens, casting out the light and leaving the world in shadow.

The gods realized their mistake, bubbles. In their desperation, they pooled all their inner radiance—their sheer energy, power, and devotion—and created someone entirely new. Out of that blinding light emerged Goddess Durga, a warrior queen of breathtaking power, riding a majestic lion. Every god gifted her a divine weapon, placing their trust in her hands.

A war raged between light and darkness for nine days and nights, but on the eighth day—Maha Ashtami—the shadow grew heavy.

Mahishasura, feeling the tide turning against him, sent two of his darkest, most cunning generals, Chanda and Munda, to strike Durga from behind. It was an act of complete treachery. But as they crept up in the dark, the Goddess felt the shift in the air.

In that exact, fleeting moment—the threshold where the eighth day bled into the ninth—Durga’s face darkened with sheer, unyielding divine wrath. From her third eye burst forth Goddess Kali, born from pure protective fury. With dark skin like midnight and eyes like burning coals, Kali stepped into the shadowed battlefield and swept away the tricksters in a single breath.

That brief, powerful moment of transition is called Sandhi Kaal. It’s the split second where dark meets light, where truth vanquishes deceit, and where love shows its fiercest, most unyielding protection.

On this same day, my sweet baby, we also remember another side of the Goddess—Mahagauri. Before the great war, she had spent centuries in deep, quiet meditation in the cold mountains, enduring severe weather until her skin turned dark and weathered. But when Lord Shiva washed her with the sacred waters of the Ganges, her true inner radiance shone through, glowing like pure white light. It reminds us that beneath every battle-worn layer, our true nature is soft, pure, and luminous.

So, bubba, what do I want to teach you today from this story?

It’s this, my love: The strongest power in the universe isn't cold, hard force—it’s the deep, protective love that refuses to let darkness win.

Sometimes, life brings moments where you feel tested, or where shadows try to creep up on you. But inside you, you carry both the calm, luminous light of Mahagauri and the fierce strength of Kali. You don't ever have to be afraid of the dark, because when you act out of pure love and truth, even the hardest battle gives way to light.

I just love you so much, baby. ♡
